## Inventory rules used in the site

- A size string containing `+` means separate selectable inventory variants. Example: `116/122 см + 6/7 г.` becomes two sizes, each temporarily with quantity 1.
- A size written with `/` but no `+` is retained as one combined label, e.g. `128 см / 8 г.`.
- Quantity defaults to 1 per size until a later physical count is supplied.
- Bonprix boys jeans `gw-0168` are explicitly marked `2 чифта`, so that size has quantity 2.
- The two Bonprix dress + bolero rows were visually the same model in two sizes; they are one product card with two size variants.
- Missing exact original/brand prices are not invented. The GERPINA selling price is still shown.
- Sold/taken products are excluded from the storefront rather than shown as sold out.
